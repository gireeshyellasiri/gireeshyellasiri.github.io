Gireesh Yellasiri

Ph.D. Candidate, Computer Science · Assistant Professor, CSE

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the National Institute of Technology Andhra Pradesh, and an Assistant Professor in the Department of CSE at Sri Vasavi Engineering College, Tadepalligudem.

My research is on video understanding: forecasting what happens next in a video, representing video compactly with continuous latent dynamics, and adapting video models to new domains with little or no labels. I also enjoy teaching, and I want to do interdisciplinary research that uses technology to solve real problems.
